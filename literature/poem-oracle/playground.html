How does a font find a poem?
The font folds your characters into a number using OpenType substitutions, then draws one of its built-in poems. The result is deterministic: a seed, not a reading of your words’ meaning. Different inputs can lead to the same poem.
Blank lines separate paragraphs. The playground sends each nonempty paragraph through the bundled HarfBuzz engine separately and draws its returned font outlines. Single line breaks within a paragraph become spaces. Everything runs locally in your browser.
In ordinary document apps, formatting and line breaks can split the input into separate runs. Copying the shaped glyphs retains your typed seed; “Copy poem text” copies the readable poem instead.
