A spelling convention, not a translator.

Arabizi varies across dialects. This font maps sounds to letters and includes 12 common word spellings, such as 7abibi → حبيبي. Other words use literal letter mappings; short vowels are not inferred.

Other consonants map to their usual Arabic equivalents; c → ك and x → خ. Capitals are preserved above; S, D, T, Z and H select the emphatic / dotted alternatives shown here. Whole-word shortcuts are lowercase and match complete words only. See the full mapping and word list.

The general alphabet uses fixed-width Arabic cells. More than 32 tokens in a word falls back to the original Latin. Punctuation and unsupported characters remain visible and break words. This preview lays out words right-to-left with separate LTR font runs. Ordinary LTR documents retain LTR word order. The font does not convert stored text to Unicode Arabic; accessibility APIs expose the source. Direct Arabic input is outside this font’s character coverage.
