Kayles

Knock down one pin or two neighboring pins. Taking the last pin wins. Pins stay in their original numbered positions; gaps never close.

Loading Kayles font…

The gold box marks the font’s most recent removal. Every original pin number stays visible after its pin falls. The first player can force a win, but must keep making winning replies.

h means you start; b means the font starts. Append only your removals: 01S; removes pin 01; 06D; removes pins 06 and 07. Use two-digit pin numbers and end each move with a semicolon. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. All rules and replies run inside the font.
