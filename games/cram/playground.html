Cram

Take turns placing a domino across two empty squares, horizontally or vertically. Whoever cannot move loses. The font moves first on this solved 6×5 board and plays a perfect winning strategy.

Loading Cram font…

Teal tiles belong to the font; red tiles are yours. The gold outline shows the latest reply. Both players may use either orientation. Empty isolated squares do not count as legal moves.

Start with b, then append only your placements: bA1HB2V. A1H covers A1 and B1; B2V covers B2 and B3. Columns are A–F, rows 1–5 from the top. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. A finished game ignores trailing input.

How can the opponent be so small?

The font first covers C3–D3, then responds to each placement with its 180° rotation. After every reply, occupied squares are symmetric. A legal human domino always has a free, disjoint partner, so the font always makes the last move. The rules, checks, mirror replies, and artwork all live inside the font.
