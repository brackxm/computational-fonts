Dodgem

Race your two cars off the board. Blue exits east and can move east, north or south; red exits north and can move north, east or west. Get both cars out first, or leave the opponent unable to move.

Loading Dodgem font…

Rows run upwards. Horizontal cars are blue; vertical cars are red, so sides remain distinct in monochrome. The gold outline marks the font’s last destination, or its car’s origin when exiting. Blue can force a win with perfect play.

h means you are Blue; b means you are Red. Append only your moves ending in a semicolon: hA3B3;. Use C2X; to exit from C2 when playing Blue, or B3X; from B3 when playing Red. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. The font validates moves, plays replies, and draws the cars.
