Snake · 20×11

A 20×11 board with its size fixed inside the font.

0 / 128 moves

Use the arrow keys, WASD, or the keypad digits 2 / 4 / 6 / 8.

Collect all eight food targets. Each pickup grows the snake and places the next food along a fixed circuit inside the walls. If its cell is occupied, food waits until the snake clears it. A crown marks the final win; an X marks a wall collision, reversal, or body collision. This build evaluates up to 128 moves. Undo lets you recover from a mistake.
