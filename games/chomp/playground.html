Chomp

Bite a square and all chocolate above and to its right. The red A1 square is poisoned: whoever is left with only A1 loses.

Loading Chomp font…

Rows run upwards from the poisoned bottom-left corner. The gold outline marks the font’s last bite. The font plays perfectly on either side; the first player can force a win.

h means you start; b means the font starts. Append only your bites as coordinates ending in a semicolon: hC3;. Spaces, tabs and line breaks are allowed. Finished games ignore trailing input. The font validates moves, chooses replies, and draws the chocolate.
