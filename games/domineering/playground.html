Domineering

Cover two empty squares with a domino. The first player places only vertical dominoes; the second places only horizontal dominoes. The player with no legal placement loses.

Loading Domineering font…

Rows run upwards. A vertical domino extends up from its coordinate; a horizontal domino extends right. Teal dominoes are Vertical, red dominoes Horizontal. The gold outline marks the latest font placement. Vertical can force a win on this 4×4 board.

h means you play Vertical first; b means you play Horizontal second. Append only your placements ending in a semicolon: hA2V;. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. The font checks every placement, plays perfect replies, and draws the dominoes.
