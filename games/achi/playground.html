Achi

Berkeley’s three-counter Achi variant: place three counters each, then slide one along a board line to an adjacent empty point. Make three in a straight row to win, during either phase.

Loading Achi font…

Rows run upwards. The font labels the current phase and solved outlook. Gold rings mark its last destination. Sliding follows the drawn lines with no jumping. Cycles can continue indefinitely; use Offer draw to finish by agreement. The font accepts when it has no forced win. This is the three-counter variant; four-counter Achi is a different game.

h means you start; b means the font starts. Append only your moves: A1; places at A1; A1A2; slides from A1 to A2. Every move ends with a semicolon. Append = to offer a draw. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. The font handles all rules, replies, draw decisions and artwork.
