Tic-tac-toe

Make three in a row, column, or diagonal. X always goes first. Choose either side and play against a perfect opponent: it takes a win when possible and otherwise guarantees a draw.

Choose your square

Squares run left to right, top to bottom. The gold outline marks the font’s latest move.

The board, exact outcome hint, legal-move checks, and opponent all come from OpenType shaping. JavaScript passes your text and reads the font’s status. A full board without a winning line ends in a draw automatically.

h starts with you as X; b starts with the font as X. Append only your square numbers: h12768 is a complete drawn game. Spaces and line breaks are allowed; a finished game ignores trailing input. This playground accepts up to 512 source characters.
