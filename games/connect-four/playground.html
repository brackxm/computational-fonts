The history is the game.

The board, gravity, move checks, opponent choices, and four-in-a-row detection all happen inside OpenType shaping. Your browser sends the human move history to the font in one run. Undo removes the last digit and shapes it again.

A complete first-player strategy.

This font uses an independently generated and exhaustively verified strategy. It guarantees a Red win from the empty board; it does not solve arbitrary positions or seek the quickest win. Full columns stop replay without a reply. Undo to continue.
