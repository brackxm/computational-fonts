Notakto

Three boards. Both players place X. A line of three closes a board; whoever closes the last board loses. The font moves first and plays perfectly. Its latest move has a gold outline.

Start with b, then write only your moves: bA2B9. Boards are A–C; squares are 1–9, left to right, top to bottom. The font inserts its own replies and draws the position. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. A finished game ignores trailing input.

JavaScript passes text and reads the font’s status. The rules, legality checks, exact winning strategy, and board artwork are all inside the font.
