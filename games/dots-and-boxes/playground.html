Dots and Boxes

Draw an edge between neighboring dots. Completing a box claims it for you and earns another move. When all twelve edges are drawn, the player with more of the four boxes wins.

Rows run upwards. First and Second show the box scores. A solid teal disk marks a first-player box; a red ring marks a second-player box. Gold marks the font’s latest edge. If you complete a box, submit your next edge yourself; the font automatically plays all of its own bonus moves. Perfect first play wins 3–1 on this board.

h means you start; b means the font starts. Append only your edges: A1H; draws right from A1; B2V; draws up from B2. Each edge ends with a semicolon. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. The font handles legality, scoring, bonus turns, perfect replies and artwork.
