Slide a counter along a line into the empty point. Trap all your opponent’s counters to win. You have four solid red counters; the font has four teal rings and plays perfectly. The standard start is a draw with perfect play.

Outer points are 1–8, clockwise from the top.
The centre, pūtahi, is point 9.

Centre-entry rule: an outer counter may enter the empty centre only if one of its two neighbors on the outer ring belongs to the opponent. Sliding around the ring or out of the centre has no such restriction. No jumping or capturing. The gold outline shows the font’s latest destination.

The font prints the exact outcome with perfect play. It accepts your draw offer when it cannot force a win. It can keep replaying a drawing loop without an artificial turn cutoff.

h starts with your move, b with the font’s. Append only the point you move from: h194289. The single empty point determines the destination. = offers a draw. Puzzle headers are w and l. Spaces and line breaks are allowed; a finished game ignores trailing input. This playground accepts up to 4,096 source characters.
