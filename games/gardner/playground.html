Gardner minichess

A playable opponent inside an OpenType font. White uses a bounded engine-generated opening book, then a legal capture-and-safety heuristic. It can make mistakes and can be beaten.

Chess on a 5 × 5 board. The font takes White; you play Black. Checkmate wins, stalemate draws. Pawns move one square, capture diagonally, and promote on the far rank. There is no castling, en passant, or double pawn step.

White pieces are outlined; Black pieces are solid teal. White moves up toward rank 5, Black down toward rank 1. Choose a promotion piece only when your pawn reaches rank 1. The gold box marks the destination of the font’s latest move.

b starts with the font as White. Append only your moves, using uppercase coordinates and a semicolon, such as bB4A3;. Add Q, R, B, or N before the semicolon when promoting. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. The font applies moves, chooses replies, draws the board, and ends games; JavaScript only edits the text and reads the font’s status.

Checkmate, stalemate, basic insufficient material, and an automatic draw after 75 moves by each side without a pawn move or capture are supported. Repetition draws and fifty-move draw claims are not implemented. The playground accepts up to 2,048 source characters.

An optional research variant and build instructions use a separately preserved certified strategy corpus, with two unproved openings. This lightweight opponent remains the default.
