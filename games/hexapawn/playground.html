Hexapawn

Three pawns each. Advance one square, or capture diagonally forward. Reach the far rank, capture every enemy pawn, or leave the opponent unable to move. White moves first; perfect Black play wins.

Loading Hexapawn font…

White pawns are outlined and move up, from rank 1 toward rank 3. Black pawns are solid teal and move down. The gold box marks the font’s latest destination. There are no double steps, en passant, or draws; captures are optional.

The font plays perfectly as either color. Let it take White if you want a chance to win: Black’s starting position is won, but you still need the right replies.

h makes you White and b makes the font White. Append only your moves, such as hB1B2C1B2, using uppercase coordinates. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. A finished game ignores trailing input. All legality, winning replies, and board drawing happen inside the font.
