Sim

Six dots, fifteen connections, two colours. Take turns colouring an unused edge. Complete a triangle in your own colour and you lose. Only triangles between three labelled dots count; crossings are not vertices.

Loading Sim font…

First-player edges are solid teal; second-player edges are red double lines, so the sides also differ in monochrome. The second player can force a win with perfect play. The font plays perfectly on either side.

h means you play first; b means the font starts. Append only your edges, with the earlier letter first and a semicolon: hAB;CD;. Spaces and line breaks are allowed. The font checks edges, plays its replies, detects losing triangles and draws the board.
