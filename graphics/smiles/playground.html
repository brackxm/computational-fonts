Font input:

Up to 10 atoms across all fragments, two branch levels and ring digits 1–3. Bonds: - = #. Atoms: B C N O P S F Cl Br I H; aromatic: b c n o p s. Brackets: optional H / H0–H4 and charges ±1–3.

How to read this diagram

Carbon atoms occupy the corners and ends of lines; other elements keep their labels. Simple rings use regular polygons, fused rings share an edge, and spiro rings share one vertex. Double bonds sit inside ring edges. Aromatic rings have a circle inside their solid polygon outline. The font supports simple rings with a single side chain and the supplied linked, fused and spiro layouts. It does not check valence, infer hydrogen labels, or show stereochemistry. Lowercase b c n o p s mark aromatic atoms. An omitted bond between two aromatic atoms is aromatic; an explicit - stays single. Bracket atoms support explicit hydrogen counts and charges, as in [nH], [NH4+] and [O-]. Hydrogen counts use subscripts; charges use superscripts. Separate disconnected fragments with ., for example [NH4+].[Cl-]. Each fragment gets its own layout with no connecting bond. Close branches and rings before each dot. Isotopes and atom maps are unsupported. Benzene accepts c1ccccc1 or C1=CC=CC=C1.

What does the browser do?

It checks syntax and supported ring connectivity in each fragment, then adds braces around your text. OpenType substitutions and mark attachment in the font track the current atom, branch stack and ring origins, then emit positioned atom and bond glyphs. The browser does not parse coordinates or draw molecule paths. Copying the diagram preserves the original SMILES text and braces.
