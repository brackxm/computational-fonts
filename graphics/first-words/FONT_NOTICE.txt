The compiled First Words Pictures fonts are modified derivatives of
Roboto Regular from googlefonts/roboto-2, tag v2.138:
https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto-2/tree/v2.138/src/hinted

The original text outlines and their notices are retained. The family is
renamed; 100 original picture glyphs, vector colour layers, monochrome
fallbacks, and whole-word OpenType substitutions are added.
