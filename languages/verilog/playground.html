Computational fonts · Verilog

Circuits, shaped by a font.

Choose a compiled Verilog module and change its inputs. Boolean gates inside the OpenType font compute all output ports in one shaping call.

Use decimal, hexadecimal (0x), or binary (0b). Inputs wrap to their port widths; negative inputs use their low two's-complement bits.

Loading the shaping engine…

This is a bounded two-state combinational subset: signed/unsigned signals, combinational blocks, module hierarchy, unrolled loops/generate, functions and small arrays. Reads outside a vector or array and division by zero are reported as errors. Sequential logic, clocks and X/Z values are unsupported. Disable font computation to verify that unfinished outputs are rejected.
